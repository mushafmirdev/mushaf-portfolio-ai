About

Building practical AI

I’m an Artificial Intelligence student and AI Developer based in Islamabad, Pakistan. I focus on building practical AI systems and exploring how emerging technologies can solve real-world problems.

My current interests and hands-on work center on Generative AI, AI Agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Machine Learning, and AI-powered applications. I primarily work with Python and have experience with large language models, AI APIs, computer vision, natural language processing, automation workflows, and web-based AI systems.

I enjoy taking an idea from a simple concept to a working prototype—whether that means building a RAG chatbot, integrating an AI model into an application, developing an automation workflow, or experimenting with computer vision and machine learning.

What I’m working with

  • Generative AI and LLM applications
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
  • AI Agents and workflow automation
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Computer Vision and NLP
  • Python and AI application development

Beyond development, I’m actively involved in technology communities and initiatives that promote AI, digital skills, and technology opportunities in Pakistan. Through community work and student-led initiatives, I enjoy helping others learn, connect, and explore opportunities in technology.
